Tropika Publisher

Internal tool of the YouTube channel Tropika VI.

Tropika Publisher is a private, internal script used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Tropika VI (French gaming news). It runs on the owner's own computer and uses the YouTube Data API v3 to upload the channel's own videos, set their title, description, tags and thumbnail, and schedule their publication date.

  • Single user: the channel owner. No public sign-up, no other accounts.
  • Only the owner's own channel and videos are accessed.
  • No data is sold, shared, or sent to any third party.

FR : Tropika Publisher est un outil interne de la chaîne YouTube Tropika VI. Il sert uniquement à son propriétaire pour envoyer et programmer ses propres vidéos via l'API YouTube Data v3.
